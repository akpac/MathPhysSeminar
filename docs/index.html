Welcome to the USN-TNM MathPhys Seminar Webpage!

This is a new seminar series under the Faculty of Technology, Natural Sciences and Maritime Sciences at the University of South-Eastern Norway, that is USN – TNM. The idea is to give blackboard (whiteboard) lectures in three of the campuses at USN: Kongsberg, Porsgrunn, Bakkenteigen (Vestfold). The date and speaker for seminar nr. n+1 will be decided at seminar nr. n. The time will always be Wednesdays 14.15 – 16 and the lectures should be scientific in mathematics, physics, or mathematical physics. This seminar can be viewed online, to obtain the link to join the seminars, please contact one of the organizers.

Upcoming Seminar

  • Wednesday 16.09.2026 at 14:15 USN, Campus Kongsberg

    • Seminar by Daniel Larsson (USN)

    Inseparable covers of schemes and non-commutive geometry.

Abstract: Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $p>0$ and let $f: X\to Y$ be an inseparable morphism of schemes of degree $p$. That the morphism is ”inseparable” means that $f$ is a homeomorphism, but the rings of functions are different, to a large extent governed by the Frobenius morphism $t\mapsto t^p$. However, using non-commutative geometry I can show that this is a mirage: one can construct a non-commutative space that ”resolves” this morphism to extract that there actually $p$ ”non-commutative points" in the fibre, even though the morphism $f$ is 1-to-1 as a commutative object. In addition, these new ”points” have a remarkable infinitesimal structure and if $Y$ is singular, these non-commutative points act as a "non-commutative resolution of singularities", since the fibre has finite global dimension (which is equivalent to being non-singular in the commutative world). This is ongoing work and I hope to write it up in a somewhat presentable (as a paper) form soon.
